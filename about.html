About Spendlens

We built Spendlens because subscription costs are silently draining bank accounts everywhere.

Our Mission

Spendlens helps you take back control of your recurring expenses. In a world where every app wants a monthly fee, it's easy to lose track. We built Spendlens to give you a clear picture of where your money is going — and where it doesn't need to go.

How It Works

Add your subscriptions once. Spendlens normalizes costs, detects overlapping services in the same category, calculates your total monthly and yearly spend, and shows you exactly where you can save money — all stored privately in your browser.

Privacy First

All your data stays in your browser's localStorage. We don't have servers, we don't collect your data, and we don't track you. Spendlens is built to serve you, not to monetize your information.
